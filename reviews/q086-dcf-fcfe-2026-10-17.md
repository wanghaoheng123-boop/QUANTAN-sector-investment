# Q-086 — the DCF is an FCFE model, says so, and refuses what it cannot value

**Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Kind:** migration note · **Accountant gate:** `reviews/q086-accountant-2026-10-17.md` returned APPROVE WITH CHANGES, and B1–B5 are resolved below. The re-gate on `c12c3b2`, `reviews/q086-accountant-regate-2026-10-17.md`, returned **APPROVE** with no blocking items; its should-fixes are handled in the final section.

## What changes for users, stated precisely

An earlier draft said "live valuation figures: no change". **That was false for every refused name** (accountant B1).

- **Names that still get a DCF:** the per-share values do not change on the four tickers measured (AAPL, JPM, NEE, XOM, 2026-10-16). Their balance-sheet rows carry only `endDate`, so the old net-debt bridge already subtracted 0. A ticker whose rows still carry debt and cash fields would have had debt subtracted before and no longer does, so its value rises. That case was not measured across the universe; the claim is "unchanged on the tickers measured", not "unchanged everywhere".
- **Names that are refused:** the DCF anchor is removed, and so are the composite **bands, the signal and the band leg of the research score**. Before this PR those names got a DCF value, and the bands, signal and score built on it. After it, the Valuation tab says why there is no DCF, and the Summary tab says why there is no band. The accountant's probe showed fair value moving in both directions (100.0 → 112.5 and 125.0 → 112.5) when the composite fell back to the two remaining anchors. That is why no band is drawn from them now (B5): the forward-earnings anchor is roughly the price itself.
- **Measured on the backtest universe (2026-10-17, a local probe that recorded no vendor values):** all five Financials (BRK-B, JPM, V, MA, BAC) are refused. The five equity REITs (PLD, AMT, EQIX, WELL, SPG), AAPL and XOM are admitted. The reporting currency matched the quote currency for all twelve.

## Why

1. **The cash flow is treated as cash to equity.** `fcf0` is Yahoo's `financialData.freeCashflow`. yahoo-finance2 types it with no definition, and nothing available to this platform verifies what the vendor deducts. An earlier draft called it "levered … after interest, so already net of debt service" as fact. That claim is withdrawn (B4). The model *assumes* it is cash available to equity holders with zero net borrowing (Damodaran 2012, ch. 14: FCFE at the cost of equity gives equity value directly), and the UI says it is an assumption.
2. **The net-debt bridge was the FCFF bridge applied to that cash flow,** and it was inert on the tickers measured. It is removed. `runDcf` is now model-neutral: `netDebt` is for FCFF callers only, and its docstring says that subtracting it under FCFE counts the debt twice.

## The refusal (Damodaran 2012, ch. 21: for financial-service firms debt is raw material, so free cash flow cannot be separated from financing)

It fails **closed**, as the refusal-gate rule in CLAUDE.md requires (B2). Classification is read field by field, because `summaryProfile: {}` used to hide a populated `assetProfile`. If both sector and industry are unavailable, the DCF is refused.

The list is an **allowlist** inside Financial Services (B3), because a denylist has to know every lender label:

| Credit Services, Asset Management | DCF | refused as **mixed labels**: the label *can* cover lenders or leveraged vehicles as well as fee businesses, and no field separates them. That membership is the accountant's recollection and is unverified, so it is not stated as fact. V and MA lose their DCF; that is the fail-closed cost. |

It also refuses when `financialCurrency` differs from the quote currency (S7). It refuses as well when the reporting currency is known but the quote currency is unknown (re-gate N2). A per-share value in TWD compared with a USD price is not a valuation. The quote currency comes from the route's `quote()` row, because the route does not request the quoteSummary `price` module. A first version read only `quoteSummary.price`, so the check could never have fired in production. `__tests__/api/fundamentalsDcfRefusal.test.ts` exercises the check through the real route handler, and removing the pass-through fails that test.

## Scenario construction (S1, S2, S4)

- The rate offsets are named: `BEAR_RATE_DELTA` 1.5pp and `BULL_RATE_DELTA` 1pp. The bull rate is `min(base, max(base − 1pp, terminal growth + MIN_RATE_SPREAD))`. The old absolute 6% floor pinned the bull value for every input from 4% to 7%, and put the bull rate *above* base at 4–5%.
- `MIN_RATE_SPREAD` is 2pp. The terminal multiple is 1/(r − g), so 2pp caps it at 50×; closer than that, the Gordon value diverges (the accountant measured 105,000/sh at 5.01% vs 5%). The DCF is refused with a reason. The 2pp is this platform's choice of cap. The rate deltas are inherited unchanged from the previous code. Neither is claimed as a sourced estimate.
- Each row now shows its rate and its terminal-value share. A note appears when the growth inputs are not ordered bear ≤ base ≤ bull, or when the bull rate cannot go below base.
- Every null carries a reason: no shares, no positive FCF (the OCF − capex fallback no longer returns a negative), the spread guard, or the refusal. `dcf.unavailable` holds it.
